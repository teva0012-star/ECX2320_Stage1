A one-day indoor market where nothing is bought or sold for cash — attendees trade clothes, plants, gear, food, tools, tattoos, tutoring and favours directly with each other. Your ticket buys your arrival slot, not your goods.

"I've got stuff I don't wear or use, but selling it online is slow, and buying secondhand still costs real money."

How it works
Three steps, no listings to write
STEP 01

Book your arrival slot

Pick a time-tiered ticket, or a stallholder space if you're bringing a full rail or table of goods to trade.

STEP 02

Trade face to face

Browse the floor and swap directly with other attendees — clothes for plants, tutoring for tattoos, whatever you can agree on. No app, no algorithm, no shipping.

STEP 03

Walk out even

Leave with something you actually want. No transaction fees, no listing photos, no waiting for a stranger to reply.
