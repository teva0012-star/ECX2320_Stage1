How billing works

Do I have to pay to trade on TradeFair?
No. Browsing, listing and swapping items or services is free, forever. You only pay if you choose one of the optional services on this page.
How am I charged for a listing boost?
Listing boosts are charged to your saved payment method at the moment you buy them. For single listings, your listing is promoted until it is taken down, sold, or been up for more than 6 months. All listing boosts last for one year after the purchase date and have the option for reoccuring payments.
How does the 2.5% cash surcharge get paid?
It's deducted automatically from the sale before your balance is paid out.
How is shipping charged?
Shipping is charged to the provider after they have recieved a shipping label matching the packages details. Payment details must be provided before a shipping label can be created.

Trust & safety

No off-platform contact. Sharing phone numbers, emails or social media handles is prohibited and automatically blocked by our chatbot monitors, to protect your privacy and safety.

Meet safely. Addresses and meeting locations can be shared through TradeFair, but we recommend using our shipping service or meeting in a public space. TradeFair isn't liable for what happens once you interact outside the TradeFair chat.
